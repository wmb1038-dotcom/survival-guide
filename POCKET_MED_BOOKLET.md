### **[PAGE 2] LEPTOSPIROSIS (FRESHWATER BACTERIA)**
**⚡ ACTION STEPS:**
1. **STERILIZE ALL WATER:** Use 0.1-micron filter or rolling boil.
2. **WOUND COVER:** Seal all cuts with waterproof film before mud/water entry.
3. **ANTIBIOTICS:** Start Doxycycline (100mg 2x daily) immediately upon symptom onset.
4. **HYDRATE:** Follow strict 1L/hour ORS protocol if fever persists.

**🚩 RED FLAGS (EVACUATE):**
* **Eyes/Skin turning yellow (Jaundice).**
* **Urine is dark, bloody, or has stopped.**
* **Coughing up blood or gasping for air.**

### **[PAGE 3] CORAL CUTS & TROPICAL STAPH**
**⚡ ACTION STEPS:**
1. **SCRUB HARD:** Use soap, fresh water, and a soft brush to remove polyps.
2. **FLUSH:** Use 3% Hydrogen Peroxide or Betadine immediately.
3. **DRY & COVER:** Apply breathable dressing. Change 3x daily.
4. **TRACK:** Circle the redness with a pen to monitor spreading.
5. **DRAW OUT:** Apply warm, moist compresses for 20m, 4x daily.

**🚩 RED FLAGS (SURGICAL LOGIC):**
* **Red streaks moving toward the heart.**
* **Center of wound is soft/squishy (Abscess needs drainage).**
* **Skin feels "crunchy" like bubble wrap (Gas Gangrene).**

### **[PAGE 4] HEAT STROKE (HUMID COOLING)**
**⚡ ACTION STEPS:**
1. **STRIP:** Remove all clothing immediately.
2. **WET & WIND:** Mist skin and use battery/manual fan (Evaporation is key).
3. **GREAT VESSELS:** Apply ice/cold rags to **Groin, Armpits, and Neck.**
4. **SALTWATER:** Drink 1 tsp salt + 6 tsp sugar per 1L water. No plain water.

**🚩 RED FLAGS (CRITICAL):**
* **CONFUSION:** Patient is aggressive, drunk-acting, or unconscious.
* **DRY SKIN:** Patient has stopped sweating despite the heat.
* **SEIZURES:** Neurological failure; protect airway.

### **[PAGE 5] FISH POISONING (CIGUATERA)**
**⚠️ PREVENTION:**
1. **AVOID:** Large reef predators (Barracuda, Roi, Ulua).
2. **DISCARD:** Never eat the Head, Liver, or Roe.

**⚡ ACTION STEPS:**
1. **CHARCOAL:** Take Activated Charcoal if fish was eaten <4 hours ago.
2. **MONITOR:** Track heart rate and blood pressure hourly.
3. **DIET:** Stop all alcohol, nut, and seed consumption for 3 months.

**🚩 RED FLAGS (HEART/LUNG):**
* **Heart rate drops below 50 BPM.**
* **Fainting or severe difficulty swallowing.**
* **Respiratory paralysis (Patient cannot breathe).**

### **[PAGE 6] RAT LUNGWORM (PARASITES)**
**⚠️ PREVENTION:**
1. **SUBMERGE:** Soak greens in 5% vinegar or salt water for 10 mins.
2. **BOIL:** Cook all local greens to 145°F (63°C).

**⚡ ACTION STEPS:**
1. **DETACH:** Shake soaked greens vigorously to dislodge slugs.
2. **INSPECT:** Use a flashlight to check the "folds" of leafy greens.
3. **CONTROL:** Trap and remove rats and slugs from garden areas.

**🚩 RED FLAGS (MENINGITIS):**
* **NECK STIFFNESS:** Patient cannot touch chin to chest.
* **SENSITIVITY:** Light hurts eyes; skin feels like "electric shocks."
* **ALARM:** Sudden loss of coordination or coma.
